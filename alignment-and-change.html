Alignment and change: a reminder from 510

You met the organizational alignment model in 510. The videos that follow use it in one way: alignment tells you what to change, and it tells you what a change has thrown off. This page is a quick reminder.

The chain

  1. 1Purpose and ambition
  2. 2Strategy
  3. 3Core capabilities
  4. 4Culture
  5. 5OrganizationStructure, systems and processes
  6. 6People

Each link has to support the one before it.

Use one: decide what to change

A gap between two links shows what needs to change. A change that does not restore alignment deserves skepticism.

Use two: check after a new initiative

Every initiative disturbs other links. After a project is introduced, ask what it has pushed out of alignment. The answers are the next changes.
