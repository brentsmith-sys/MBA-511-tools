Project Planning

A three-step exercise: rank on your own, rank with your team, then see how you did.

The situation

Your organization has just assigned you to a newly formed task team which is taking over a secret project presently being handled by Research and Development. Your entire team has been assigned responsibility and authority to design a plan for managing the project, and then—after top management has reviewed and accepted your plans—carry out the project.

None of you has been told anything about the project so far, other than it is expected to grow to sizable proportions, requiring additional people.

The challenge

Despite the lack of information regarding the project, your team must now design a preliminary plan for managing it. Below are 20 Management Activities (A through T). Your task is to arrange these activities according to the sequence you would follow in planning, organizing, implementing, and controlling the project. This sequence will be reviewed by top management before you are authorized to begin work on the project.

You will not see a score in this step. Your score is revealed in Step 3, after your team has finished its own ranking. When you finish here, you will get a copy of your ranking to keep for the team session.
